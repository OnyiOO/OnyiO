Project Highlights

Fall 2024

Using an MSP-EXP430FR2433 and Code Composer Studio, I created a pulse width color modulation circuit. I selected colors which could be created through percentage mixtures of red, green, and blue LEDs. For example, turquoise, shown below, can be represented when red is 40% on, green is 80% on, and blue is 80% on. The switches on the MSP430 are connected to each LED and are ticked in increments of 10% until the color is achieved. The duty cycle shown in the second image controlled the intensity of the color shown on the LED, where 10% is a dim white light and 90% is a very intense color.

I personally contributed by creating the website animation, populating website content, and working on bug fixes. Check out krfrank4.github.io/buckIT/ to test it for yourself!

Fall 2025

Using Verilog coding and a Terasic DE10-Lite board, my team of three created a hexadecimal clock counter. When the assigned switch was pressed, the board displayed numbers from 0 to F on the Hex scale, then reset to zero when pressed once more. I was in charge of drawing up the Karnaugh maps to implement the logic used and creating the looping effect on Verilog.

Spring 2026

In my longest project yet, my team of five created a working distortion pedal over the course of a semester. First, I created the circuit design on Multisim and employed an oscilloscope to ensure a properly clipped output. Then, myself and another team member assembled the components on a breadboard. After the breadboard output was also confirmed, myself and another team member created a PCB on Fusion and printed it in our school's lab. I was in charge of populating the PCB by soldering the components from the breadboard. Then a member 3D printed a pedal box and the pieces were brought together. I received an A from my professor for my contribution to this project.
